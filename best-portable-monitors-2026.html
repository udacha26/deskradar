Which one should you get?

If you want the most screen for the least fuss and don't mind the extra size, the ARZOPA 17.3" gives you the largest display area with flexible USB-C or HDMI input. If budget and portability matter most, the Ingnok 15.6" is the lightest and simplest option of the four. If you do colour-sensitive work or just want a noticeably sharper, smoother picture, the cocopar QHD 120Hz is the only one that offers it — check the current price, since higher specs usually mean a higher price tag. If you want a recognisable brand name and a single-cable setup for a laptop that already supports USB-C video and charging, the Lenovo L15 is the most "plug and forget" office option, provided your laptop is compatible.
