Converter or full electric desk — which one should you get?

If you already have a desk you like and just want to add standing capability without buying new furniture, a converter (VIVO 32", VIVO 36" or HUANUO 32") is the simpler and cheaper path — none of them need a power outlet, and all three set up in minutes on top of an existing desk. Between the three, the VIVO 32" has the widest height range, the VIVO 36" has the most desktop surface for a dual-monitor setup, and the HUANUO 32" includes a full keyboard-and-mouse tray as standard.

If you want a single, permanent standing desk with programmable height presets and a much higher weight capacity, a full electric desk (CubiCubi or Cubiker, both 40"×24", 155 lb capacity) is the stronger option — but it requires more floor space and a nearby outlet, and neither manufacturer's listing confirms single- vs. dual-motor lift, so don't assume premium build quality without checking the current listing yourself.
